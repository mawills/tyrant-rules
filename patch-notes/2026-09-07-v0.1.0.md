# TYRANT v0.1.0 Patch Notes

## Core Rules

- Instead of two cards per refresh phase, each player is dealt a number of tyrant cards equal to the current Act during each tyrant phase e.g. one in Act I, two in Act II, and three in Act III.

- Allies now suffer unpreventable Consequential Damage when performing basic actions. The amount suffered is printed on the card next to the stat associated with each basic action. These are referred to by their shorthand ACD (attack consequential damage) and QCD (quest consequential damage).

*Designer Note: Allies were far too powerful compared to any other type of card when they can stay around and provide value turn after turn. Allies can still stick around longer if healed, but this now forces players to decide between healing themselves or healing allies to squeeze additional value out of them.*

- Added the **reclaim** ability keyword: Return a card from your discard pile to hand. Some reclaim effects may specify specific cards or types of cards that can be reclaimed. If no type of card is specified, you can return any card from your discard pile to hand. If there are no eligible cards in your discard pile, then this has no effect.

- Added the **tough** ability keyword: While a character has tough, whenever they would take ⚔️, prevent 1 of that damage. Multiple instances of tough do not stack.

## Quests

All quests now add 💀 to make tyrant quest progress more smooth and predictable. Higher Acts will add greater amounts of 💀.

*Designer Note: Previously, the tyrant's quest progress was added, on average, at a constant rate throughout the game while the players are generally able to increase the rate at which they progress the quest, easily outpacing the tyrant in later Acts. This change should make the tyrant scoring later Acts more of a threat.*
