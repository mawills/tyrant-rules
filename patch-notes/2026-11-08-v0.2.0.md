# TYRANT v0.2.0 Patch Notes

## Core Rules

The quest system has received a complete overhaul. Quest cards have been removed, Acts are removed, and the tyrant can no longer score quests. Instead, ten specific cards from each hero deck (5 from the core deck and 5 from the specialization) are now Quest Rewards and marked in the top right with a ! symbol. At the beginning of the game, these cards are set aside in a separte pile.

Players still accumulate quest progress in the same way as before, but instead accumulate their quest progress counters to purchase their Quest Rewards. At all times, the top two cards of the deck are visible and can be claimed for a number of quest progress counters equal to their cost. When a quest reward is claimed, it is immediately played, the other visible quest reward is placed on the bottom on the pile, and two new quest rewards are revealed.

Since there are no longer Acts, the tyrant scales its power in a simpler way: at the beginning of the game, the tyrant deals two cards to each player each round. Every four rounds, the number of cards dealt increases by 1.
