# TYRANT v0.3.0 Patch Notes

Across the board, Ally HP and consequential damage has been doubled. It wasn't particularly valuable to ever use Allies to block damage and this change is intended to make that use more appealing. Because conseuqential damage has also been doubled, the number of "uses" Allies have for basic attacking and questing should remain unchanged.

Added a book symbol in the top-right corner of Innate cards to more easily identify them.

## Tyrant

Rebalanced the damage profile of the tyrant deck to flatten the damage spikes of Attacks and make incoming damage more consistent overall. In general, the damage of Attacks has been slightly reduced and the ATK of Minions slightly increased.

- Arrogance: Damage decreased 5⚔️ (+5⚔️) -> 4⚔️ (+4⚔️)
- Balefire: Damage decreased 3⚔️, 3⚔️ -> 2⚔️, 2⚔️
- Bombard: "3⚔️ all heroes, 1⚔️ all allies" -> "3⚔️ all enemies"
- Corrupted Elemental: ATK increased 1 -> 2
- Paincaster: ATK increased 1 -> 2
- Stone Sentinel: ATK increased 1 -> 2
